About The Foundation

Empowering the next generation through direct, transparent grassroots action.

Rooted in community, driven by purpose.

The Agbewornu Support (T.A.S.) Foundation was built by a core group of individuals dedicated to bridging gaps in our local communities. We believe that true development happens from the ground up—through direct engagement, resource sharing, and equipping the younger generation with the awareness and tools they need to thrive.

Whether we are running educational outreach sessions for Junior High School students or mobilizing support resources, our focus is always on creating genuine, lasting impact without the red tape.
